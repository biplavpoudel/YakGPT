Real Trekking Knowledge Collected
Altitude Medicine
Climb High, Sleep Low
The core principle of Himalayan acclimatization. Ascend to a higher altitude during the day, then return to sleep lower. This stimulates red blood cell production without overwhelming the body.
AMS Warning Signs
The Lake Louise Score
The standard clinical tool for diagnosing Acute Mountain Sickness. Headache is the required primary symptom. Add nausea, fatigue, dizziness, or poor sleep for severity scoring.
Thorong La Timing
Cross Before 10am
Afternoon storms at Thorong La are common and can be deadly. Trekkers starting before 4am have significantly lower turnaround rates. The pass does not wait for late sleepers.
